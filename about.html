02 / About Me

The engineer behind the vision.

Hi, I’m Prayas Das — a final-year B.Tech Electrical Engineering student at Ghani Khan Choudhury Institute of Engineering & Technology (GKCIET), Malda, Class of 2027, with a CGPA of 7.909 (up to 6th semester).

My foundation comes from a Diploma in Electrical Engineering at Falakata Polytechnic (CGPA 8.20/10). Since then I have built hands-on expertise in embedded systems, IoT and machine-learning-driven control for electric vehicles, working in C, Python and MATLAB with tools like ETAP, PSIM, ANSYS and AutoCAD Electrical.

I am a first author on two published research papers, presented at PEIS 2026 (Malda) and ICVCS 2026 (Kolkata), and I completed an internship at the Electric Locomotive Shed, Malda in 2026. On campus I serve as Student Coordinator for the Training & Placement Office, the Finishing School Program and the E-Cell.

I’m looking for roles in electrical power systems, embedded systems, VLSI and other design-driven engineering work.
